About Us

History

Dian was a poor young boy with big dreams of becoming a successful businessman. His mother loved making homemade donuts and had a secret recipe. Before she passed away, she taught Dian the recipe and encouraged him to follow his dreams. Inspired by his mother, Dian decided to start a small donut shop called Donut Eats. He was joined by four helpers: Rane, Mynhard, Az, Zia, and Darwynah, who helped him prepare, sell, and manage the donuts. At first, the business struggled, but thanks to their teamwork, hard work, and delicious donuts, more customers started visiting. Soon, Donut Eats became a popular shop. As their business grew, they introduced new flavors, hired more workers, and opened additional branches. From a small donut shop, Donut Eats became a successful business built on teamwork, determination, and Dian's mother's special recipe, and they hope to branch out all over the world.
